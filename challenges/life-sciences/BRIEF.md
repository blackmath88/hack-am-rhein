# Discover the Basel Life Sciences Ecosystem

> Make Basel’s life-sciences ecosystem navigable.

**Domain:** life-science

## Original challenge wording

Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft form one of Europe’s densest life-sciences clusters, bringing together global pharmaceutical companies, biotech startups, research institutions, investors, laboratories, accelerators and specialist service providers.

Yet for a founder, researcher or growing company, finding the right resource at the right time can still be difficult.

Can you make the Basel life-sciences ecosystem intelligently searchable, connected and actionable?

Build a solution that helps a startup or research team understand who can help them, where they should go and what they should do next.

A company might say:

> “We are a six-person oncology startup preparing for Series A. We need BSL-2 laboratory space, regulatory expertise, potential investors and pharma partners.”

Your solution should turn that need into useful recommendations, connections or next steps across Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft.

What could you build? Ideas include an ecosystem navigator, startup roadmap generator, investor or mentor matching engine, talent marketplace, laboratory and infrastructure finder, collaboration platform, interactive ecosystem map, knowledge graph, or an agent that creates a personalized “next 12 months in Basel” plan for a startup.

> **The goal is not another directory. Build something that helps people make decisions and take action.**
