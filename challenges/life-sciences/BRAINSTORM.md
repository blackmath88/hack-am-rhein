# Brainstorm — Basel Life Sciences Ecosystem

## Problem beneath the brief

The ecosystem already contains organisations, directories, programmes and infrastructure. The deeper problem may be **navigating the ecosystem at the right moment**.

## Broken decision

> Given who we are and where we are, what is relevant now, what should happen next, and why?

## Early framing

Possible semantic object:

```
STARTUP STATE
→ NEEDS
→ ELIGIBILITY
→ PREREQUISITES
→ MATCHES
→ DEPENDENCIES
→ NEXT ACTIONS
→ EVIDENCE
```

Potential dimensions:

- company stage
- therapeutic / technology domain
- team size
- funding stage
- lab / infrastructure need
- regulatory need
- investor relevance
- pharma / partner relevance
- prerequisites
- timing
- dependencies

## What AI might be for

- interpret fuzzy founder language;
- infer candidate need categories;
- explain matches;
- detect missing information;
- help turn a changing startup situation into a typed case.

## What should stay explicit / deterministic

- source facts;
- eligibility rules where available;
- organisation identity;
- resource capabilities;
- provenance;
- known prerequisites;
- accepted / rejected matches.

## Ideas

### Ecosystem case rather than directory

A startup gets a living case showing:

- NOW
- NEXT
- PARALLEL
- BLOCKED
- LATER

Every recommendation answers **why this**, **why now**, and **what unlocks it**.

### Open questions

- Can enough eligibility / programme data be obtained to make recommendations defensible?
- Are relationships and introductions public data, inferred data, or human-curated data?
- Is “next 12 months” actually useful, or should the product focus on the next decision?
